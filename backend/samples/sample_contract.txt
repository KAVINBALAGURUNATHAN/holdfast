1. SCOPE OF WORK

The Freelancer shall design and build a responsive e-commerce website for the Client's organic food products, including a product catalogue, cart, Razorpay checkout and an admin panel, as described in the milestones below.

2. PROJECT FEE AND PAYMENT TERMS

The total project fee is INR 1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only), exclusive of GST at 18%, payable in three milestones. Each milestone amount shall be placed in escrow by the Client before work on that milestone begins, and released within 7 working days of the Client accepting the deliverable against the acceptance criteria below. TDS under Section 194J, if applicable, shall be deducted by the Client.

3. MILESTONES

Milestone 1: UI/UX Design and Clickable Prototype
Amount: Rs. 30,000
Due Date: 20/12/2026
Description: Wireframes and high-fidelity designs for all key pages, delivered as a clickable Figma prototype.
Acceptance Criteria:
- Figma prototype covers home, product listing, product detail, cart and checkout pages
- Designs are responsive with mobile and desktop layouts for every page
- Brand colours and logo from the Client's brand guide are applied consistently
- Client feedback from one review round is incorporated

Milestone 2: Frontend and Backend Development
Amount: ₹75,000
Due Date: 31 January 2027
Description: Development of the working website on a staging server based on the approved designs.
Acceptance Criteria:
- Product catalogue with search and category filters works on staging
- Cart and Razorpay checkout complete a test payment in test mode
- Admin panel allows adding, editing and deleting products
- Lighthouse performance score of at least 85 on mobile for the home page
- Source code is pushed to the Client's private GitHub repository

Milestone 3: Deployment, Training and Handover
Amount: INR 45,000
Due Date: 15 February 2027
Description: Production deployment, documentation and handover.
Acceptance Criteria:
- Website is deployed on the Client's production domain with HTTPS enabled
- Admin user guide document is delivered in PDF format
- One live training session of at least one hour is conducted for the Client's staff
- 30-day bug-fix support period is confirmed in writing

4. ACCEPTANCE AND REVIEW

The Client shall review each deliverable within 5 working days of submission. If the deliverable does not meet the acceptance criteria, the Client shall provide written reasons, and the Freelancer shall have 7 days to remedy the shortfall. Deliverables not reviewed within the review period shall be deemed accepted.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Upon full payment of each milestone, all intellectual property rights in the deliverables for that milestone shall vest in the Client.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party during the term of this Agreement and for two years thereafter.

7. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 15 days' written notice. Escrowed amounts for milestones already accepted shall be released to the Freelancer; escrowed amounts for milestones not yet started shall be refunded to the Client.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute shall first be referred to mediation, failing which it shall be resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the seat of arbitration at Bengaluru.
