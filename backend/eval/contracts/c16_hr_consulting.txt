HR CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Shree Balaji Textiles, Surat, through its owner Mr. Mahesh Patel, and Dr. Anjali Bhatt, HR consultant, Vadodara.

Client: Shree Balaji Textiles
Consultant: Dr. Anjali Bhatt

The Consultant will help the Client set up formal HR policies for its 180 employees.

Stage 1 - Policy Manual. The first payment of ₹2.25 lakh becomes due when the Consultant delivers a complete HR policy manual. The manual must cover leave, attendance, code of conduct and the POSH policy required under the 2013 Act, and it must be reviewed and signed off by the Client's owner. Target date for Stage 1: 20 January 2027.

Stage 2 - Rollout. The second payment of ₹75,000 becomes due on completion of rollout. Rollout is complete when the Internal Committee under the POSH Act has been constituted and trained, and when every employee has received the manual in Gujarati or Hindi. Target date for Stage 2: 25 February 2027.

The Client will reimburse printing costs of the manual separately.
